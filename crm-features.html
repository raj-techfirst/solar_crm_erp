Inside the CRM

What the sales team works with

Every module below is part of the same customer and project record, so nothing is retyped when the deal moves forward.

Lead Management

Capture enquiries with source, location, capacity and consumer number, then assign an owner and a follow-up date.

Master & Panel Master

Panel companies, panel types, wattage, inverter companies and item groups maintained once and reused everywhere.

User Roles & Access

Roles and permissions for owner, manager, sales, store, installer and accounts, with user status and division views.

Quotation & Estimates

Residential with subsidy, trading and solar rooftop quotation types, backed by the rate calculator and estimate PDFs.

Sales Order & Handover

Convert an accepted quotation into a sales order, then carry project application, documents, meter application and payment collection forward.

Follow-Ups & Site Visits

Search and filter leads, record follow-ups, tasks and site-visit notes, and move the deal through the status master.

Inquiries Dashboard

One dashboard for open enquiries, completed leads and pending work, filtered by division, city, taluka and user.

Reports & Commission

Sales order and payment reports, agent-wise performance, commission lists and payment-pending views with Excel and PDF export.

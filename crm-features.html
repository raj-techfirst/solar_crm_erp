The pipeline

Nine stages from enquiry to completion

Every enquiry, quote, order and installation is one continuous record, so nothing is retyped and nothing falls between teams as the deal moves forward.

Lead Capture

Enquiries arrive from the public enquiry form, sales staff add them by hand, or a whole list is brought in by Excel import — each with source, location, capacity, consumer number and an assigned owner. The mobile app captures on the same record.

Funnel & Lead Statuses

A configurable funnel runs New Lead, Inprocess, Qualified, Site Visit Scheduled, Site Visit Done, Sales Quotation Sent and Negotiation, closing as Lead Won or Lead Lost — plus On Hold, MW Lead and Land Buy or Rental for large deals.

Follow-Ups & Rate Given

Every call, remark, status change and image is written to a follow-up audit trail, so the history of a deal is never guesswork. Rate Given holds a per-lead price list that feeds the quotation line items directly.

Site Visit

Book the visit, assign a site visitor, and capture remarks and photographs from the roof. The field team works the pending and completed lists from the mobile app, and it all stays on the lead record.

Estimate & Quotation

Cost the job as an estimate with a printable PDF, then quote it properly as Resident With Subsidy, Solar Rooftop or B2B — with panels, inverters, PV capacity, meter charges, technical specifications and negotiation revisions.

Sales Order

An accepted quotation converts into a sales order carrying consumer GST, Aadhaar, bank and DISCOM details, supply phase and cash-or-loan type, with the agent's commission snapshotted at the moment of conversion.

Payments & Collection

Record cash, cheque, NEFT, UPI, RTGS, discount, adjustment and loan disbursement against the order. Approving a receipt reduces the pending amount and advances the order; putting it on hold or returning it rolls the milestone back.

Dispatch & Installation

Approved orders queue on a delivery challan built from the bill of materials, with serial numbers tracked per issue. Once dispatched, the installer records panels, inverters and site photos, and stock is consumed against the project.

Meter, Subsidy & Completion

Meter application and meter installation, then the subsidy claim and receipt on eligible projects before the order closes as complete — generating self-certification, request letters, agreements, declarations and net-metering paperwork as it goes.

Under the hood

Four status engines, one pipeline

The flow is not one long status list. Four independent engines run at once, and the pipeline is simply the order they advance in.

A configurable lead funnel carries every enquiry from New Lead through to Lead Won or Lead Lost. A quotation carries its own five-state machine, from active to accepted, revised, cancelled or dispatched. The sales order tracks each milestone as its own flag in a fixed sequence, and every payment carries an approval state that both reads from and writes to those milestones.

  • Dispatch is never set by hand. A quotation is only marked dispatched when a delivery challan is issued against it, and editing or deleting that challan returns it to accepted automatically.
  • One quotation converts to one sales order, and only once. A quotation that has already been converted is never offered again.
  • Single-phase projects skip the paperwork. Once the registration is approved, feasibility and meter charge are cleared automatically, with no DISCOM round trip.
  • Loan orders branch out. Cash and loan files share the same pipeline, but a loan order picks up apply, sanction and disbursement between meter charge and payment received.
  • Subsidy is applied only where it applies. Projects on a GEDA connection, or national-portal customers who have given up the subsidy, close from meter installation straight to completion.
  • Milestones can be reversed. A wrongly flagged step is cleared and re-set, and any single status change is written to the follow-up audit with the old and new value.

Users & access

Everyone sees the data their job needs

Access follows the reporting line, not a flat permission list. Every list, report and dashboard query is scoped the same way, so a number on screen is always a number that user is allowed to see.

Owner

Sees every lead, quotation, order and payment in the company, across all managers and teams. The only level that can cancel a sales order, and the level that owns the role and permission settings.

Manager

Sees their own records plus every lead and order belonging to the sales people reporting to them. The team pipeline, agent performance and commission figures are scoped the same way.

Sales

Sees the leads they created and the leads assigned to them, with their own follow-ups, quotations and orders. Assigned work is visible to the field team on the mobile app at the same time.

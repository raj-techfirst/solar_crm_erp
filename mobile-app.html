Why it works on a phone

Built for a rooftop, not a desk

A solar business spends most of its day away from a keyboard. The app is designed around that, and it still feeds the same records your office sees.

Works offline

Load the day's leads and projects before you leave, then capture visits, photos and serials with no signal. It syncs when you are back in range.

Photos where they happen

Site photos, installation evidence and damage shots attach to the record as you take them, not in a folder of screenshots later.

Scan, don't type

Serials and item codes are scanned with the camera. No typing a twelve-digit number on a dusty keyboard in the sun.

Role-based access

A salesperson sees their own leads. A store keeper sees stock. A site engineer sees his checklist. Everyone sees the same project.

The modules

Six modules that cover store to site

Each one is usable on its own and better together. They share the same item master, so a receipt, a challan and a report are always talking about the same thing.

Warehouse Stock

The central ledger for every item you trade. Book inward from a receipt, outward against a challan, and let opening and closing balance themselves.

  • Item-wise ledgers
  • Opening & closing auto-computed
  • Low-stock flags

Purchase Order

Raise a PO against a project or a supplier with agreed rates and terms, then track exactly how much is still outstanding.

  • Supplier-wise POs
  • Pending quantity tracking
  • Rate & terms on record

Good Receipt

Book material in against a purchase order, or raise a direct GRN when a supplier delivers without one. Serials are captured at the point of entry.

  • PO-linked GRN
  • Direct GRN without PO
  • Serial capture at receipt

Delivery Challan

Send material to site with the serials listed on the challan. Record what the installer actually consumed and close the loop on anything returned.

  • Project-wise dispatch
  • Return challan
  • Site confirmation

Projectwise Stock

Stock reserved, issued and consumed for each installation, kept separate from the main warehouse so a busy project cannot hide a shortage.

  • Reserved vs issued
  • Per-project requirement view
  • Shortfall alerts

Report & Analysis

Turn the ledger into the numbers you actually review in a Monday meeting, then export them for your accountant without rebuilding anything.

  • Stock history by item
  • Purchase rate variance
  • Export to Excel

Every serial has a history you can read

A panel that arrived on the 4th, sat in the warehouse for nine days and was installed on the 13th is a warranty question waiting to happen. Tracking serials from inward through dispatch to site means a recall or a service call is a lookup, not an investigation.

  • Serial numbers captured at goods receipt
  • Same serials printed on the delivery challan
  • Installation consumption confirmed on site
  • Warranty claims start from a real trail

  • Delivery challan — 13 Mar Dispatched on DC-0341, acknowledged by the site engineer.

Warehouse stock and project stock are different questions

“How much do we have?” and “How much does this installation still need?” fail you in opposite directions. A healthy warehouse can still leave a project short, so the ERP answers both separately — and flags the gap before the crew is standing in a bare terrace.

  • Central ledger stays independent of any project
  • Requirement, issued and consumed tracked per project
  • Shortfall raised before dispatch is planned
  • Adjustments recorded, never silently overwritten

Report & analysis

The four reports that run a solar business

Not a hundred exports to choose from. These are the ones worth having open, and every one of them is a live view of the same ledger.

Stock History

Every inward, outward and adjustment for an item, with the running balance, so a wrong quantity can be traced to a date.

Required Stock

What the approved BoM calls for, against what each project has actually received. The report that catches a short site.

Purchase Analysis

Spend by supplier, item and month, with rate variance against your own master, so margin leaks show up early.

Dispatch Summary

Challans issued, material consumed and returns pending, grouped by project and ready to export for your accountant.

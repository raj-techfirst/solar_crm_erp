The project lifecycle

One thread, from first call to AMC

Every stage writes back to the same project, so sales, store, site and accounts never argue about which version is correct.

01 — Enquiry

Lead captured once with source, capacity, location and consumer number, then assigned to an owner with a follow-up date.

02 — Site survey

Roof, structure, load and shadow findings recorded during the visit, so feasibility is a decision and not an opinion.

03 — Quotation

Residential, subsidy, commercial and trading quotes built from your own item master, with approval and revision history.

04 — Order & finance

Accepted quote becomes a sales order. Subsidy documents, loan sanction, disbursement and EMIs hang off that order.

05 — Dispatch & install

Material leaves against a delivery challan with serials, and installation confirms what was actually consumed on site.

06 — Net metering & AMC

Meter application, subsidy claim and closure continue after commissioning, with warranty keyed to installed serials.

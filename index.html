Why this holds together

The thread, not the spreadsheet

Most solar businesses do not lose money on a bad day. They lose it on the gap between what sales promised, what the store issued and what actually got installed. A single project record closes that gap: every stage writes back to the same customer, project and item master, so nobody has to reconcile two versions of the truth.

  • One customer, one owner

    A lead keeps the same owner, site and consumer number from the first call through the service request.

  • Status engines, not free-text notes

    Leads, projects, serials and stock each move through their own defined set of states, so a stage means the same thing to everyone.

  • kW-wise bill of materials

    The quotation, the dispatch challan and the installed system share one component list, so a change in capacity flows through to what you buy.

  • Serial-level service history

    Warranty and AMC are answered from the serial that was actually installed, not from a memory of which customer had which complaint.

The project lifecycle

One thread, from first call to AMC

Six working moments. Inside each one, the CRM and the ERP are both updating the same project, which is why a dispatch can hand material over without anybody re-keying it.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    A lead is captured once with source, capacity, location and consumer number, then assigned to an owner with a follow-up date.

  2. 02

    Site survey

    Roof, structure, load and shadow findings are recorded during the visit, so feasibility becomes a decision instead of an opinion.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    Residential, subsidy, commercial and trading quotes are built from your own item master, with approval and revision history kept.

  4. 04

    Order & finance

    The accepted quote becomes a sales order. Subsidy documents, loan sanction, disbursement and EMIs hang off that order rather than a side spreadsheet.

  5. 05

    Dispatch & install

    Material leaves against a delivery challan with serials, and installation confirms what was actually consumed on site.

  6. 06

    Meter, subsidy & AMC

    Meter application, subsidy claim and closure continue after commissioning, with warranty keyed to the installed serials.
